02 — Talking points

What to say about it

The angles that land. Pick the one that fits your voice — you don't need all of them.

AThe one-liner

"It's the doomscroll that actually makes you smarter — every tech headline from the sources I trust, in one feed, filtered to the topics I care about."

BThe problem it kills

Stop opening six news sites and twenty tabs. TechScroll pulls TechCrunch, Wired, The Verge, Ars Technica & SiliconValley.com into one clean stream — no algorithm rage-bait, no autoplay junk.

CThe killer feature

Type a keyword — "AI", "funding", "OpenAI", "nvidia" — and the whole feed filters live across every source at once. It's a personal tech-news search engine.

DWho it's for

Founders, devs, VCs, PMs, and anyone who needs to sound informed in the standup without losing an hour to the timeline. Built for people who are busy but curious.

EWhy it feels good

Fast, dark, distraction-free. No infinite ads, no clickbait ranking, no "you won't believe." Just headlines, sources, and a tap-through to the real article.

FThe honest note

It's an independent reader — not affiliated with the publishers. It links straight back to them. Great line for credibility: "It respects the source, it just saves you the chaos."

03 — Content ideas

Ready-made video & post concepts

Hooks and formats that work for short-form. Each is a full concept you can shoot today.

⏱️ "5 minutes, fully caught up"

Screen-record yourself scanning the feed for 5 min before coffee. Voiceover the 3 stories that matter. Hook: "This is how I stay on top of tech without doomscrolling for an hour."

🆚 "6 tabs vs 1 app"

Split screen: messy browser with TechCrunch/Verge/Wired tabs on one side, TechScroll on the other. Hook: "POV: you finally stopped opening six news sites every morning."

🔍 "Type one word, watch this"

Film the live keyword filter. Type "AI" → feed snaps to AI stories across every source. Hook: "It's basically a search engine for tech news and it's free."

🧠 "Apps every founder should have"

List format — slot TechScroll as "the one that keeps me informed." Hook: "3 apps I open before I check Slack."

📲 "Rate my home screen"

Casual home-screen tour; spend 10s on why TechScroll replaced your news app. Low-effort, high-relatability.

🌙 "Doomscroll, but make it productive"

Lean into the doomscroll meme, then flip it: "same dopamine, but I actually learn something." Great for a relatable, funny tone.

05 — Brand assets

Logo, colors & the rules

Use these so it always looks like TechScroll. Keep the bolt on a dark background.

The mark

TechScroll

Wordmark is one word, camel-case: TechScroll. The ⚡ bolt is the icon. Always pair with the dark background — don't put it on white.

Palette

Accent#00e08a
Sky#34c5ff
Violet#a98bff
Ink#070a0e

The signature look is the green→sky→violet gradient on near-black. That's the vibe to match in thumbnails.

Quick do / don't

  • Do call it an independent news reader / aggregator.
  • Do say headlines link back to the original publisher.
  • Do show the live keyword search — it's the wow moment.
  • Don't imply it's made by or partnered with TechCrunch, Wired, etc.
  • Don't claim it hosts full articles — it links out.
  • Don't recolor the bolt or put the logo on a light bg.
