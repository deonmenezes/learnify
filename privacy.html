Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

TechScroll ("the app", "we") is a tech-news reader that shows headlines from public sources and links you out to read the full article on the publisher's own website. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

TechScroll is designed to collect as little as possible. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers, and we do not sell your personal data.

  • Account details (email address and optional display name) and your topic interests are stored locally on your device to power sign-in and personalize your feed.
  • Saved articles are stored locally on your device.
  • Marketing (opt-in only): if — and only if — you tick the optional "updates" box at sign-up, we collect your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your phone number, in order to send you product updates and tech-news digests. This is off by default, is never required to use the app, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We do not sell this information.

Marketing communications

If you opt in, we use your email (and optional phone number) only to send you TechScroll updates and digests. Every message includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also email privacy@techscroll.app to be removed at any time.

News content

To populate your feed, the app requests public news metadata (headlines, source names, categories, links) from our content endpoint. These requests do not include your account information or any personal identifiers. Article headlines and links belong to their respective publishers; TechScroll links you out to read full articles at the source.

Tracking

TechScroll does not track you across apps or websites and does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission.

Children

TechScroll is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@techscroll.app.
