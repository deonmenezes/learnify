Common questions

How do I pick my topics? On first launch, choose at least three topics. You can change them anytime in Settings → Manage interests.

How do I read the full article? Tap any story (or "Read on …") to open the full article on the publisher's website.

How do I save a story? Tap the bookmark icon on any card; find saved stories in the Saved tab.

How do I delete my account? Open Account & Privacy from the subscription screen, or choose Settings → Delete Account after subscribing. Deletion permanently removes your account and associated synchronized data from our servers, then clears saved items, likes, and learning progress from the device. If your session expired, Learnify asks you to sign in again before authorizing deletion.
